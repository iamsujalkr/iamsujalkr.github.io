🔐 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2025

Welcome to pwnCraft – Cybersecurity Blog & Portfolio of Sujal Kumar (https://iamsujalkr.github.io/). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

1. Who We Are​

This website is maintained by Sujal Kumar, a cybersecurity researcher based in India. The site is a personal portfolio and educational blog focused on cybersecurity research, tutorials, walkthroughs, and related technical content.

If you have any questions about this policy, feel free to contact us at: 📧 iamsujalkr@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect​

We do not collect personal data directly from visitors, except in the following cases:

  • Analytics Data
    We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site. This service does not use cookies or track individuals.

  • Embedded Third-Party Content
    Our pages may include embedded content (such as YouTube videos, GitHub gists, or Twitter posts, etc). These services may collect user data such as IP address, browser type, and interaction behavior, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Contact Page (Future Use)
    If a contact form is added in the future, we may collect your name, email address, and message solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiries.

3. How We Use Your Information​

All collected data (currently only analytics) is used for:

  • Understanding site traffic and performance
  • Improving content and user experience
  • Displaying third-party media or embeds as intended

We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties.

4. Cookies​

This website does not set or use cookies directly.

However, some embedded content (such as YouTube) may place cookies on your device. These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third-party platforms.

5. Third-Party Services Used​

We currently use the following services:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics
    A privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies or track personal identifiers.

  • Embedded Services

    • YouTube (for video content)
    • GitHub (for code previews and gists)
    • Twitter/X (for embedded posts, if applicable)

These services may collect visitor data when their content is displayed or interacted with.

6. External Links​

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, such as blogs, security tools, or external resources we find useful.

Please note:

  • These websites are not under our control.
  • We are not responsible for their content, accuracy, or privacy practices.
  • Visiting those sites is at your own discretion.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites they visit through external links on our site.

7. Data Storage and Retention​

  • This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • We do not store any personal data on our servers.
  • All analytics data is handled by Cloudflare in accordance with their privacy standards.

8. Your Rights​

If you are located in the EU, California, or other regions with data protection laws, you may have rights related to your personal data — such as access, correction, or deletion.

Since we do not directly collect or store personal data at this time, these rights generally do not apply. However, you can contact us at any time with privacy-related questions.

9. Changes to This Policy​

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future to reflect new features, technologies, or legal requirements.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
